02. PROBLEM LANDSCAPE
The High Cost of The Fine Print
The Problem
  • Commercial leases span 20–80 dense pages. Manual review is slow, error-prone, and leads to costly oversights.
  • Missing 180-day non-renewal notice windows forces predatory auto-renewals.
  • Overlooking strict subletting prohibitions triggers default legal fees.
  • Ignoring complex HVAC upkeep structures shifts severe maintenance liabilities onto tenants.
The Solution
  • LeaseSight introduces a hybrid RAG pipeline that answers natural language questions with pixel-precise evidence grounding.
  • Instant plain-English query resolution with source-anchored highlights.
  • 768-dimensional dense vector proximity ranking via all-mpnet-base-v2.
  • Multi-agent risk audit automation (Miner → Judge → Clerk).

06. INTELLIGENCE LAYER
The Single-Pass Audit Engine
01
The Miner
Extracts a 20-point compliance matrix covering Parties, Dates, Rent, Deposits, Late Fees, Governing Law, and Termination clauses. Each finding is anchored with a verbatim evidence_quote string directly from the contract text.
02
The Judge
Benchmarks extracted terms against statutory regulations — specifically Section 74 of Pakistan's Contract Act 1872 (liquidated damages and penalty provisions). Assigns a 1–10 risk index and generates flagged warning arrays.
03
The Clerk
Serializes multi-agent legal logic and all extracted variables into a clean, schema-compliant JSON payload for real-time dashboard injection, visual bounding-box grounding, and audit PDF generation.

10. CRITICAL DIAGNOSIS
Anatomy of a Failure
Stress Test Incident
RETRIEVAL_ERROR — Clause Split Boundary
Root Cause
High-value data fragments across physical page breaks. Page 4 contains descriptive legal headers and is successfully retrieved, but the actual percentage value sits on Page 5 and drops below the hardcoded top_k=5 cosine similarity ceiling.
Mitigation Strategy
Transition from rigid per-page partitioning to Advanced Sliding-Window Token Chunking with a 128-token overlap parameter to guarantee clause boundary continuity.
